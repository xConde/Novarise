Battlefield rendering unavailable

Novarise needs WebGL to render the battlefield. Your browser or device reported that hardware-accelerated graphics are not available right now.

  • Enable hardware acceleration in your browser settings (search for "hardware acceleration" in your browser's preferences).
  • Update your browser to the latest version — Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari all support WebGL.
  • Update your GPU drivers if the issue persists after updating the browser.
  • Try a different browser if driver updates are not possible on your machine.
